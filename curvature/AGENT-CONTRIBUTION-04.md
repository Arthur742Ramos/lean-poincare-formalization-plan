# Historical Submission 04 contribution record

This document is retained as a historical record of the superseded
coordinate Ricci–DeTurck/metric-cone candidate. It is not the current Palomar
surface. The current geometric transport candidate and its role/oversight
record are documented in `AGENT-CONTRIBUTION-05.md`.

The superseded candidate was intentionally withdrawn from the active
Comparator after review identified that its Ricci data were not geometrically
tied to an evolving metric and that its research-interest case was too weak.
Its exact files and commit remain available in repository history; they are
not described by the current `Challenge.lean`, `Solution.lean`,
`comparator.json`, or `formalization.yaml`.
